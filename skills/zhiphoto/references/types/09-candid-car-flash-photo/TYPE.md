# Candid Car-Interior Flash Photo

Compose an authentic photograph captured from inside the same vehicle by a nearby friend, preserving the user's exact subject, identity, pose, clothing, props, framing, and emotional direction. The defining visual system is the enclosed car interior, direct phone flash, low ambient exposure, and an unplanned private moment.

This type is for a separate-camera or phone-camera snapshot taken by someone nearby in the car, not a selfie or mirror selfie. Read the required foundation, then apply the selected scene profile only as a delta. Subjects must be unmistakably adults; use age 25+ when an adult age is unspecified.

Do not invent a default nationality, ethnicity, gender, body type, hairstyle, outfit, or degree of revealing clothing. Do not turn an ordinary brief into glamour, eroticism, or a fashion editorial. Preserve requested alcohol, food, devices, or other props, but do not add them when the brief leaves them open.
