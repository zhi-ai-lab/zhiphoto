# Studio High-Key Beauty Selfie

## Use When

The request calls for bright, controlled beauty lighting and cleaner styling while retaining selfie perspective.

## Profile Deltas

- Use a large soft frontal or slightly off-axis source with gentle fill, bright background values, clear catchlights, and shallow but present facial modeling.
- Emphasize intentional makeup, hair, jewelry, and neckline details requested by the user without losing pores, fine facial hair, or natural contours.
- Keep the phone-at-arm's-length viewpoint evident through crop and perspective even when the finish is polished.
- Use a simple tonal background or subtle studio falloff that supports the face rather than competing with it.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Avoid blown-out facial planes, shadowless flatness, ring-light halos in every eye, waxy retouching, or a catalog headshot composition.
- Avoid replacing user-requested character with generic beauty-ad perfection.
