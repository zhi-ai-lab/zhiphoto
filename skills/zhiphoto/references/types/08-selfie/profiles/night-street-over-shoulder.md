# Night Street Over-the-Shoulder Selfie

## Use When

The request centers on a walking-away, turn-back, or over-the-shoulder moment in a nighttime public setting.

## Profile Deltas

- Build the pose around a readable torso turn, shoulder line, head rotation, and backward glance, keeping neck and camera-arm geometry plausible.
- Motivate face light with nearby shop windows, street lamps, vehicle lights, or neon; allow mixed color temperatures and dimmer shadow-side detail.
- Use receding street depth, scattered practical lights, and modest background motion to suggest an active city without turning it into a neon fantasy.
- Permit slight phone-camera noise, flare, motion softness, and bright practical-light clipping while keeping the eyes and expression legible.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Avoid impossible spine, neck, shoulder, or camera-arm rotations.
- Avoid evenly lit daylight skin, excessive cyberpunk color, empty streets without explanation, or razor-sharp detail at every depth.
