# Casual Social Selfie

## Use When

The request should feel personal, immediate, and shareable rather than commissioned or editorial.

## Profile Deltas

- Use an ordinary lived-in background such as a cafe table, bedroom corner, kitchen, campus walkway, or neighborhood stop, with only a few readable contextual objects.
- Favor relaxed posture, an unforced half-smile or situational expression, comfortable styling, and a crop that feels chosen quickly.
- Allow small signs of the moment: a displaced hair strand, uneven garment fold, beverage, bag strap, friend partly entering frame, or mild ambient clutter.
- Keep polish consistent with an appealing phone post, not a campaign image.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Avoid influencer-ad staging, perfectly arranged props, luxury cues not requested, conspicuous product placement, or a professional studio backdrop.
- Avoid making every casual detail messy; the image should feel lived-in, not neglected.
