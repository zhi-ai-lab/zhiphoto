# Outdoor Place Selfie in Daylight

## Use When

The setting or landmark is part of the story and daylight conditions shape the face and atmosphere.

## Profile Deltas

- Keep the subject dominant while leaving enough background to recognize the requested place through its silhouette, materials, landscape, or spatial layout.
- State the sun direction in image coordinates when given, then propagate it to facial highlights, cast shadows, hair rim light, skin sheen, eye squint, and background exposure.
- Match expression and eye openness to the brightness; strong side or frontal sun can produce a believable partial squint without looking sleepy.
- Add restrained environmental motion such as breeze-touched hair, moving pedestrians, foliage, flags, or water only when plausible.
- Let the place remain naturally imperfect: ordinary visitors, partial occlusion, atmospheric haze, or a slightly clipped bright sky can improve credibility.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Avoid compositing the person and landmark at conflicting scales, focus distances, sun angles, or color temperatures.
- Avoid postcard-wide framing that makes the selfie subject incidental, and avoid pristine empty landmarks unless requested.
