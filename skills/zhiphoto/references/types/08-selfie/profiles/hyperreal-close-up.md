# Hyperreal Unretouched Close-Up Selfie

## Use When

The request centers on a face-dominant selfie or self-portrait whose main appeal is truthful skin texture and a natural photographic finish, rather than a specific age, gender, or polished beauty identity.

## Profile Deltas

- Keep the face as the clear visual subject in a tight vertical 9:16 crop, while preserving a believable self-portrait relationship between camera viewpoint, gaze, pose, and expression.
- Use naturally uneven skin texture: visible pores, fine vellus hair, subtle redness around the nostrils, mild under-eye shadow, and believable skin translucency. Retain ordinary asymmetry and small imperfections.
- Use a neutral dark background with gentle blur and a soft directional side key that reveals facial planes and skin relief without flattening the shadows.
- When the request specifies an 85mm lens or portrait-lens look, prioritize that compressed, flattering perspective over the mild wide-angle geometry of a typical phone selfie; do not add visible phone or arm details unless the requested viewpoint needs them.
- Keep color true to life with cinematic but restrained contrast, eyes critically sharp, and the rest of the face falling off naturally into shallow depth of field. Describe the result as a real photograph, not CGI or a beauty render.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Do not default to any age, gender, or other demographic attribute the user did not request.
- Avoid skin smoothing, beauty filters, plastic texture, poreless retouching, excessive makeup-ad gloss, or synthetic HDR.
- Avoid flat frontal or ring-light illumination, busy backgrounds, wide-angle facial distortion, and implausible selfie anatomy.
- Avoid turning the close-up into an editorial headshot when the user clearly wants an informal selfie; preserve the requested level of spontaneity and directness.
