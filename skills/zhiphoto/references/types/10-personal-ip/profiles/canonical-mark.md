# Canonical Personal IP Mark

## Use When

Use this profile when the user wants one compact, reusable visual identity from a person's reference photo, especially for an avatar, profile image, or small-scale recurring character.

## Profile Deltas

- Generate one complete 1:1 square image with one human-derived character on a named solid background.
- Use a compact head-and-shoulders or upper-body crop with a strong readable silhouette. Center the character or let it emerge from a lower corner when that better supports the requested composition; do not crop away a paired identity feature.
- Reduce the person to four to seven large rounded shapes, two simple eyes, and only the facial details needed to preserve the selected identity anchors.
- Preserve the person's recognizable hair, face silhouette, glasses, facial hair, or signature headwear from the reference, while designing the outfit from the written role brief rather than copying incidental photo clothing.
- Use exactly two purposeful character color families plus the background by default. Keep the background visually uniform and clearly separated from the character.
- Keep the person clearly adult, friendly, and approachable without childish chibi anatomy, photorealistic materials, anime styling, 3D rendering, or corporate clip-art detail.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Do not carry over poster text, captions, logos, UI, scenery, or unrelated props from a screenshot reference.
- Do not introduce laptops, coffee, microphones, or other generic creator symbols unless the user's role brief calls for them.
- Do not add text, watermark, borders, frames, extra subjects, scenery, fragile lines, sharp tips, glossy hotspots, heavy gradients, deep shadows, or external cast shadows.
