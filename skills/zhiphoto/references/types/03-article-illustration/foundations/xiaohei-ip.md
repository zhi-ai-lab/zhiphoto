# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP for Ian-style body-copy illustrations.

By default, Xiaohei appears in every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration — it is an absurd worker earnestly participating in the system's operation.

## Appearance

- A solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or a black block from inside a machine.
- The outline is slightly irregular, with a hand-drawn feel.
- The expression is blank, dull, calm, and serious.

## Personality

- Very earnest, but what it does is a bit absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Deadpan humor, never cute for cute's sake.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- As if it is genuinely responsible for some job within the whiteboard sketch.

## Common Duties

Let Xiaohei carry out the core action:

- Hauling materials.
- Pulling lines together to converge information sources.
- Stuck at a breakpoint.
- Operating the "judgment" lever inside the machine.
- Turning into a sifting funnel.
- Cutting open the "material fish."
- Stamping to seal a pitch/script.
- Leading a handoff path.
- Holding up a warning sign at a pitfall.
- Reaching a hand out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Off to the side, hauling bricks, building a bridge, opening a door, sorting, or recording.

## Prohibitions

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Do not draw it as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei elaborate clothing, emoji-style expressions, or sparkly eyes.
- Do not let Xiaohei merely stand in a corner watching.
- Do not let Xiaohei upstage the structural point being made.
- Do not draw Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, or too polished/refined.

## Judgment Standard

If removing Xiaohei would leave the image's core metaphor fully intact, Xiaohei is too decorative — rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the subject of the action.
