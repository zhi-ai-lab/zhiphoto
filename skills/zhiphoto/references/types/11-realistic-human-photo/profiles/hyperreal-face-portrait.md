# Hyperreal Face Portrait

## Use When

The request wants a close-up, face-dominant, documentary-realistic portrait where skin texture is the point of the image.

## Profile Deltas

- Default the subject to an adult woman aged 25-30 when the user does not specify one; any user-specified subject, age, or gender always wins.
- Make the face the dominant subject of the frame, composed as a tight portrait rather than a half-body or environmental shot.
- Place the subject against a neutral dark background with soft defocus so nothing competes with the face.
- Light with a soft directional key from the side, chosen to accentuate skin texture and the contours of the face; keep fill restrained so modeling stays visible.
- Specify a high-end 85mm portrait-lens rendering with shallow depth of field and critical sharpness on the eyes, letting ears and background fall softly out of focus.
- Foreground the foundation's skin truth: visible pores, fine vellus hair, faint redness around the nostrils, mild under-eye shading, and real skin translucency, with no smoothing or beauty effects.
- Keep color true-to-life with cinematic but natural contrast, and state photographic realism with no CGI-render look.
- Default to a vertical 9:16 aspect ratio unless the user requests another format.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Beauty-filter skin, glamour retouching, or makeup-advertisement gloss replacing documentary texture.
- Flat frontal lighting or ring-light catchlights that erase the side-lit facial modeling.
- Bright, busy, or recognizable backgrounds pulling attention from the face.
- Wide-angle facial distortion or selfie-style arm's-length geometry.
