# Window Portrait Realism Foundation

Use these shared decisions for every window-light-portrait profile unless the user explicitly chooses a different photographic treatment.

## Human detail

- Preserve naturally uneven skin: visible fine pores, subtle freckles, fine vellus hair, delicate lines, believable under-eye texture, lip texture, and localized variation in tone.
- Keep the subject attractive through expression, light, composition, and presence, not through smoothing, airbrushing, uniform pale skin, or exaggerated makeup.
- Retain ordinary facial asymmetry, credible anatomy, lively dimensional eyes, and real hair strands with flyaways where appropriate.

## Window light and color

- Use one motivated soft side key from the nearby window. Keep the light diffuse and let it create fine, readable transitions across the face rather than flat frontal illumination.
- If the environment is described as pale green, allow a restrained pale-green bounce or reflection to influence the shadow-side skin and nearby surfaces without tinting the entire image unnaturally.
- Keep contrast low and cinematic but natural. Highlights should remain matte-to-natural rather than oily, clipped, or glossy.

## Camera and film behavior

- For a close portrait, use a believable normal portrait perspective around the user's stated focal length, with the eyes as the critical focus plane and natural shallow-depth falloff behind them.
- Render fine 35mm film grain as a subtle photographic texture, with only a slight, restrained halation or glow around bright window edges.
- The result must read as an unretouched photograph: no CGI sheen, plastic skin, HDR crispness, or perfect symmetry.

## Shared avoidances

- Over-smoothed skin, pore removal, beauty-filter gradients, fake uniform whitening, oily specular highlights, or over-sharpened pores.
- Exaggerated eyelashes, heavy glam styling, commercial makeup-advertising polish, or a catalog beauty campaign feeling unless the user asks for them.
- Unmotivated color casts, harsh direct sun, ring-light catchlights, wide-angle facial distortion, watermarks, logos, or readable text.
