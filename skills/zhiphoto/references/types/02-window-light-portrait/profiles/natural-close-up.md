# Unretouched Window-Side Close-Up

## Use When

The request wants an intimate, face-dominant portrait beside a window where natural skin texture, soft daylight modeling, and a quiet film-photography finish are central. This profile is equally suitable for any explicitly specified or unspecified age, gender, ethnicity, or nationality.

## Profile Deltas

- Let the face occupy most of the frame in a close, calm composition. Keep the nearby window edge or softly abstracted room present enough to establish the light source without competing with the face.
- Use soft, diffused side-window light with delicate shadow detail. Add only a restrained pale-green environmental reflection into the shadow-side skin and surrounding tones when that palette is requested.
- Use a 50mm photographic perspective, shallow depth of field, and precise focus on the nearer eye; allow the farther facial plane, hair, and background to soften naturally without losing human texture.
- Grade with low contrast, true-to-life color, fine 35mm film grain, and a very slight glow around bright window areas. Keep the subject unretouched but appealing through natural light and expression.
- Preserve real pores, minor freckles, fine lines, facial vellus hair, under-eye texture, natural uneven coloration, and lip texture. Keep hair strands believable and eyelashes natural rather than amplified.

## Profile-Specific Avoidances

- Do not add an age, gender, ethnicity, nationality, or other demographic identity the user did not request.
- Avoid over-smoothed or plastic skin, oily highlights, uniform artificial whiteness, exaggerated eyelashes, heavy makeup, and commercial beauty-ad polish.
- Avoid dark studio-backdrop conventions, ring-light flattening, harsh sunlight, strong green color wash, synthetic HDR, excessive halation, watermarks, and text.
